## Sub-categories M1 (for patient) and M2 (for medical attendant)

1. **Passport**, in original, with a minimum validity of six months as on the date of submission of application for visa. The passport should have at least two (2) blank pages.
2. **Old passports** must be submitted with the application form.
3. **One recent passport-size colour photograph** (2X2) depicting full face with white background.
4. A copy of **National ID Card OR Birth Certificate** (whichever applicable).
5. **Proof of Residence**: a copy of the electricity bill OR gas bill OR landline telephone bill (not less than 6 months old).
6. **Proof of Financial soundness** — one of the following:
   - Bank statement for last six months showing sufficient funds
   - OR Endorsement of foreign currency equivalent to **US$ 200/-** (endorsement should not be older than three months at the time of submission)
   - OR copy of international credit card or Travel card (example: SBI Travel card) its deposit statement
7. **Proof of Profession** — one of the following:
   - Government Order Copy (if the applicant is a serving government employee)
   - OR Certificate from the employer (if the applicant is a private company employee)
   - OR a copy of the Registration granted to the Business establishment by the Government of Bangladesh along with an English translation [if not in English] (in case of self-employed persons)
   - OR a copy of Identity card from the educational institution (if the applicant is a student)
   - OR a copy of the retirement papers (if the applicant is a retired government employee)
8. Necessary medical documents in support of the ailments/disease from local doctor along with medical reports.
9. A **letter of appointment** from a Government / ICMR (Indian Council of Medical Research) / NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) / MCI (Medical Council of India) / CGHS (Central Government Health Scheme) **recognized hospital in India**.
10. Copy of **invitation letter** (Medical/Ayush visa).
11. For Official passport holders also, it is advisable to take medical visa as well as medical attendant.
12. **Minor applicants** have to be compulsorily accompanied by parent/guardian as medical attendant. In case the patient is a minor, then parents may apply for visa as medical attendant (M2) and any other sibling or minor child who is dependent on the M2 visa holder may apply for X-Misc visa.
13. Necessary clearance from the **Law Ministry & Ministry of Foreign Affairs (MOFA)** in case of Organ Transplantation cases (viz., Kidney, Liver, etc.).

## Sub-categories M3 (for patient taking treatment under Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy services) and M4 (for medical attendant)

Items 1–8 are the same as for M1/M2 above (passport, old passports, photograph, National ID / Birth Certificate, proof of residence, proof of financial soundness with US$ 200/- endorsement option, proof of profession, and medical documents from a local doctor). In addition:

9. A **letter of appointment** from a hospital/wellness centre accredited and registered with relevant government authority(ies) concerned or **NABH/NCH/NCISM accredited hospitals providing AYUSH** (Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy) services.
10. Copy of invitation letter (Medical/Ayush visa).
11. For Official passport holders also, it is advisable to take medical visa as well as medical attendant.
12. **Minor applicants** have to be compulsorily accompanied by parent/guardian as medical attendant. In case the patient is a minor, then parents may apply for visa as medical attendant (M4) and any other sibling or minor child who is dependent on the M4 visa holder may apply for X-Misc visa.

**Please note (from the source):** The above list of documents are not final. High Commission of India, Dhaka reserves the right to ask any additional documents for processing the visa application.
