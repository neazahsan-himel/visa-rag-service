# India Business Visa — Required Documents (Bangladeshi Passport Applicants)

**Source:** High Commission of India, Dhaka — Visa Documents: Category-wise

**Country:** India (IN)
**Passport:** Bangladesh (BD)
**Visa type:** BUSINESS
**Official category name:** Business Visa — sub-categories B-1 (general business), B-2 (sports persons and coaches), B-3 (artists / cultural personalities / academicians / professionals in commercial performances). Sub-category B-5 (conference/seminar/workshop) is covered in the Conference Visa document.

## Business Visa (B-1) — for all business activities (other than those covered by B-2, B-3 and B-4 visas), including maintenance, Repair and Overhaul (MRO) activities of aircraft and ships

1. **Passport**, in original, with a minimum validity of six months as on the date of submission of application for visa. The passport should have at least two (2) blank pages.
2. **Old passports** must be submitted with the application form.
3. **One recent passport-size colour photograph** (2X2) depicting full face with white background.
4. A copy of **National ID Card OR Birth Certificate** (whichever applicable).
5. **Proof of Residence**: a copy of the electricity bill OR gas bill OR landline telephone bill (not less than 6 months old).
6. **Proof of Financial soundness**: copy of the **Income tax returns / audited accounts for the last two years** showing **annual income not less than 5 lacs per annum**.
7. **Proof of Profession** — one of the following:
   - Government Order Copy (if the applicant is a serving government employee)
   - OR Certificate from the employer (if the applicant is a private company employee)
   - OR a copy of the Registration granted to the Business establishment by the Government of Bangladesh along with an English translation [if not in English] (in case of self-employed persons)
   - OR a copy of the retirement papers (if the applicant is a retired government employee)
8. A letter from the company/firm in Bangladesh giving details of the applicant, his/her designation and the purpose of the visit.
9. A **letter of invitation from any Indian company/firm registered with or sponsored by the appropriate authorities in India**, i.e. Reserve Bank of India, MCCI, FICCI, ASSOCHAM, CII, NASSCOM etc., clearly indicating the city to be visited and the duration of the visit.
10. A copy of membership certificate or sponsorship letter of any reputed **Chamber of Commerce** of Bangladesh or the appropriate authorities like Bangladesh Bank, Board of Investment, BEPZA, FBCCI, DCCI etc.
11. A copy of valid **Trade License with Tax Identification Number (TIN)**.
12. A copy of statement of the bank account of the company of the applicant showing **gross sales/turnover 50 lacs per annum or above**.
13. **If the applicant is an importer/exporter**, the following are required:
    - Import Registration Certificate (IRC) & Export Registration Certificate (ERC)
    - A copy of the Letter of Credit (LC) / Telegraphic Transfer (TT) (in case of exports) pertaining either to the last three business transactions or the impending business transaction with an Indian company
14. **If employed in a company/private firm**, a copy of the Employment contract (showing period of contract, designation and monthly salary of the applicant).
15. **If the applicant is participating in or visiting a trade/business exhibition/fair in India**, details of the nature of participation and a copy of the invitation letter from the exhibition/fair authorities or Federation/Chamber concerned has to be submitted.
16. **If the applicant represents a limited company**, a Memorandum of Articles of Association and Certificate of Incorporation is to be submitted.
17. **If the applicant is representing a branch office/Liaison office of a company in Bangladesh**, a relevant copy of approval from the Board of Investment under the Prime Minister's office is to be submitted.
18. **If the application is from Tourism Agencies**, their permit issued by the Ministry of Tourism, Bangladesh and updated membership of the Association of Travel Agents of Bangladesh (ATAB) is to be submitted.
19. Family members of business visa holders applying for family visa (B1-X) should enclose a copy of the visa of the Principal Visa holder, if applying separately.

## Business Visa (B-2) — for sports persons and coaches, including those engaged in commercial sports events in India on contract with remuneration

1. Passport (minimum validity of six months, at least two blank pages), old passports, one recent 2X2 colour photograph with white background, a copy of National ID Card OR Birth Certificate, and proof of residence (electricity OR gas OR landline telephone bill, not less than 6 months old) — as in B-1 items 1–5.
6. Copy of invitation letter issued by the concerned Sports Federation/Association in India.
7. Copy of clearance from the **Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, Government of India** and from the **Ministry of External Affairs, Government of India**.
8. Family members of B2 visa holders may apply for family visa (B2-X).
9. In case any player is going to India to attend a meeting of sports bodies like the International Olympic Association, International Cricket Council etc., they may apply for B-5 visa.
10. Income tax return / proof of tax-payment receipts for payments received from earlier commercial sports events attended in India.

## Business Visa (B-3) — for artists / cultural personalities / academicians / professionals who visit India to participate in cultural/commercial performances or concerts, and for those involved in performative arts like acting, dance etc.

1. Passport (minimum validity of six months, at least two blank pages), old passports, one recent 2X2 colour photograph with white background, a copy of National ID Card OR Birth Certificate, and proof of residence (electricity OR gas OR landline telephone bill, not less than 6 months old) — as in B-1 items 1–5.
6. A letter of invitation from the organizers of the event in India.
7. A letter of introduction from the organization in which the applicant is working.
8. Income tax return / proof of tax-payment receipts for payments received from earlier commercial performances in India.

**Please note (from the source):** The above list of documents are not final. High Commission of India, Dhaka reserves the right to ask any additional documents for processing the visa application.
