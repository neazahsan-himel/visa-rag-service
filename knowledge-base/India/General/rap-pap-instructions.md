## Overview

Bangladeshi citizens who hold a valid Indian visa and wish to visit a Restricted Area or Protected Area in India may apply for a Restricted Area Permit / Protected Area Permit (RAP/PAP). Passports, along with the duly filled-in prescribed RAP/PAP application form, may be submitted at the Indian Visa Application Centre (IVAC), Jamuna Future Park, Dhaka. The RAP/PAP application form is available on both the High Commission of India, Dhaka website and the IVAC website.

## Instructions for Filling the Application Form

1. Mention the specific place to be visited against Point 14 of the form — e.g. "Gangtok," not only "Sikkim."
2. If travelling in a group or with family, list all group/family members' names against Point 19 of the form.
3. Specify the exact route and mode of travel against Point 15 of the form — e.g. "Dhaka-Changrabandah-Gangtok - By Road" or "Dhaka-Kolkata-Gangtok - By Air."
4. Clearly state the date of arrival and date of departure.

## Additional Conditions

2. All foreigners visiting a Restricted/Protected area must register with the Foreigners Registration Officer (FRO) of the State/District they visit, within 24 hours of arrival.
3. Photography of vulnerable areas/points, including sensitive Army installations/equipment, is not permitted.
4. The applicant should not participate in discussions on any controversial issues.
5. The permit is valid only for the specific tourist circuit/route and the specific entry/exit point stated on it — no other area may be visited.
6. The permit holder must carry sufficient photocopies of the permit, as a copy may be required at each point of entry/exit, and must possess necessary travel documents at all times.
7. The permit holder must not remain in the restricted/protected area after the permit expires.
