## Requirements

- **Format:** JPEG
- **Size:** minimum 10 KB, maximum 300 KB
- The height and width of the photo must be equal.
- Photo must present full face, front view, eyes open.
- Head centered within frame, showing the full head from top of hair to bottom of chin.
- Background must be plain, light-colored or white.
- No shadows on the face or on the background.
- No borders.
- Head should measure 1 inch to 1-3/8 inches (25mm to 35mm).
