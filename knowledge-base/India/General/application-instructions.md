## A. Online Application Form

- The online application form is designed for foreigners applying for a visa from outside India.
- Each online application form is meant for one person only — a separate submission is required per applicant.
- Information must be provided exactly as requested, particularly names, address, and date of birth.
- Applicants must verify all details before submission; incomplete forms can be saved as drafts.
- Once the online application form is submitted, further modifications are not allowed.
- Keep the automatically generated Application ID for future communication with the Mission.

## B. Appointment Scheduling

- Each applicant schedules their own appointment with their chosen Indian Mission, according to personal convenience.

## C. Process for Filling the Online Application Form

1. Access the system at https://indianvisaonline.gov.in
2. Select the appropriate Indian Mission from the "Select Mission" tab.
3. Complete the standardized online form.
4. The system automatically generates an Application ID after submission.
5. The system then prompts the applicant to choose:
   - **"No"** — print the form and submit it, with supporting documents, directly at the Indian Mission.
   - **"Yes"** — proceed to online appointment selection, fee calculation, and e-Payment.
6. Note: "e-payment facility is not available for all the Indian Missions abroad."

## Technical Requirements

- Best viewed in Mozilla Firefox, Google Chrome, or Internet Explorer (Windows) version 9.0 and above.
- Adobe Acrobat Reader version 7.0 or higher is required to view/download forms.
- Compatible with Android and iOS for the e-Arrival Card / mobile app flow.
- Printed forms should be produced with either an Ink-Jet or Laser printer.
- The signed physical copy of the application must accompany supporting documents at the scheduled appointment with the Indian Mission.
