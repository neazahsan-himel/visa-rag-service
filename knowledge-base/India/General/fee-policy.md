## Actual Fee (Bangladeshi Passport Holders)

Per the High Commission of India, Dhaka's FAQ (see `hci-dhaka-faq.md` Q3):

> "বাংলাদেশীদের জন্য ভারত সরকার কর্তৃক কোনো ভিসা ফি নেয়া হয় না। ভারতীয় ভিসা আবেদন কেন্দ্র নামমাত্র ৮০০ টাকা প্রক্রিয়াকরণ ফি নিয়ে থাকে।"

**No visa fee is charged by the Government of India for Bangladeshi nationals.** The Indian Visa Application Centre (IVAC) charges a nominal processing fee of **৳800 (Bangladeshi Taka)**. This is the only concrete visa-fee figure found across all sources checked; it supersedes the "no fee table published" note below for the specific case of a Bangladeshi passport holder.

**Not the same as consular/passport fees:** the High Commission's published "Fee Schedule" PDF (`dhaka_29jun2026_fees.pdf`, effective 1 July 2026) covers passport re-issuance, OCI, nationality, and attestation services for Indian citizens/OCI cardholders in Bangladesh — it has no visa-fee section and does not apply to a Bangladeshi national applying for an Indian visa. Not ingested here; noted only to prevent conflating the two.

## General Fee Policy (from the two visa portals)

- "The fee depends upon the type [visa is] applied for and its duration."
- Visa fee is divided into various components depending on the case: **basic fee**, **special fee**, and **processing fee by out-sourcing agency**.
- During online registration, the basic fee is displayed, but this is **indicative only** — the final fee is charged at the time of application submission at the respective counter.
- **Refund policy:** "Fee once received are non-refundable" — even if the application is withdrawn, the visa is not granted, or the visa issued is of shorter duration than applied for, or otherwise issued/returned on terms different from those requested.
- For exact fee amounts and payment methods, applicants are directed to check with the local embassy, the Indian Visa Application Center, or the relevant website — **neither source page publishes a specific fee table or numeric amounts**.

## Remaining gap

The ৳800 IVAC processing fee above is a flat figure from a general FAQ, not broken down by visa type or duration — despite the portals' own text stating fee "depends upon the type applied for and its duration." No per-visa-type fee table has been found on any source checked so far. If a user asks about a specific visa type's fee, answer with the ৳800 flat figure and note that it's the only published figure, rather than fabricating a type-specific breakdown that doesn't exist in any source.
